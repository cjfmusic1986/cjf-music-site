2024: A Musical Odyssey

2024: A Musical Odyssey is a collaborative EP between CJF Music and Astro Rockit, an MC from Columbus, Ohio. Their chemistry was first introduced on CJF Radio Vol. 3 with the standout record "Silence of the Lambs", and this project expands that collaboration into a complete listening experience.

Blending Astro Rockit's lyrical style with CJF Music's signature boom bap production, the EP delivers hard-hitting drums, soulful samples, and creative storytelling. Each song explores a different mood while staying true to classic hip hop, making 2024: A Musical Odyssey a celebration of collaboration and timeless production.
