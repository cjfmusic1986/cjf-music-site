About CJF1986

Born and raised in Brooklyn, NY, CJF1986 is a boom bap producer specializing in soulful hip-hop production, remix albums, and collaborations with underground artists.

Inspired by legends like Jay-Z, Nas, 2Pac and Mobb Deep, his music blends vintage soul samples with modern drums to create a timeless sound.

Since 2022, CJF1986 has released multiple remix albums, original projects and collaborative EPs while continuing to build a catalog rooted in classic East Coast hip-hop.
