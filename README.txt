CJF HOMEPAGE PLAYER FIX — INSTALLATION

1. Back up your current website files first.
2. Upload index.html and styles.css to the same folder as your current homepage.
3. Upload js/music-player.js into your website js/ folder.
4. Keep your existing images/ folder and beats/ folder unchanged.
5. Confirm these exact MP3 files are present on your web host:
   beats/97' CJF.mp3
   beats/DENZEL IN TRAINING DAY.mp3
   beats/Martin Scorsese Movie.mp3
6. Open your homepage and hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).

IMPORTANT: No MP3 files are included in this package. The filenames were taken
from the beats.html you provided. If they are not uploaded to your hosting
account, track names will show but audio cannot play.

This package fixes the HOMEPAGE only. Do not overwrite beats.html with index.html.
The homepage player will stop when navigating to a new HTML page; seamless
cross-page playback would require a separate enhancement.

TO ADD MORE MUSIC LATER: Open js/music-player.js and add entries to CJF_TRACKS.
