Jay-Z – The Blendprint

The Blendprint is the project that started it all. Released in 2022, this remix album reimagines Jay-Z's classic The Blueprint album with entirely new production from CJF1986.

Built on soulful samples, hard-hitting drums, and boom bap-inspired production, The Blendprint gives one of hip-hop's most iconic albums a fresh sonic identity. This project laid the foundation for CJF1986's remix catalog and introduced a new way to experience Jay-Z's legendary lyrics.
