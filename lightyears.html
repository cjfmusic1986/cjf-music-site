Nas – Light Years (CJF Remix)

Light Years is a remix album exploring Nas’ reflective, philosophical, and forward-thinking catalog. This project focuses on maturity, legacy, and lyrical evolution pairing Nas vocals with fresh, modern boom bap inspired production.

The album highlights growth over time, personal stories, and the mindset of an artist who’s always thinking ahead light years beyond the present.
