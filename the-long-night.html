50 Cent – The Long Night

From “The Good Die Young” to “When It Rains, It Pours,” The Long Night is a raw and relentless journey through the shadows of the street. This remix album reimagines 50 Cent’s darkest tracks as chapters in a single, haunting narrative. Each song paints a piece of the night: betrayal, revenge, survival, and the burden of power.
