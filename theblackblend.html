Jay-Z – The Black Blend

The Black Blend reimagines Jay-Z's classic The Black Album with entirely new production from CJF1986. Soulful samples, boom bap drums, and fresh arrangements give these legendary verses a completely different feel.

Inspired by the spirit of the original album while creating a unique sonic identity, The Black Blend continues the remix journey that began with The Blendprint.
