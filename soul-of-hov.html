Jay-Z – The Soul Of Hov

The Soul Of Hov is a tribute to classic hip-hop, bringing brand new soulful production to some of Jay-Z's most memorable freestyles and rare performances. Produced entirely by CJF Music, the project combines vintage soul samples, gritty boom bap drums, and timeless East Coast production to create a fresh listening experience while honoring Hov's legendary lyricism.

Rather than remixing a single album, The Soul Of Hov focuses on classic freestyles that helped define Jay-Z's legacy. Each track has been completely reimagined with soulful instrumentation, giving these iconic verses a new atmosphere while preserving the energy and confidence that made them unforgettable.

  1. Young, Gifted & Black (CJF Remix)
  2. Jay-Z Freestyle (CJF Remix)
  3. Pump It Up Freestyle (CJF Remix)
  4. Murdergram Freestyle (feat. Ja Rule & DMX) (CJF Remix)
  5. Funk Flex Freestyle (CJF Remix)
  6. Outro (CJF Remix)
